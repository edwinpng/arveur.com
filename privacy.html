2. In short

This site sets no cookies and loads all of its scripts, styles and images from its own server. Personal data is processed only in the cases described below: when the site is delivered (hosting), for anonymous visitor statistics, and when you email me.

3. Hosting: GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: my legitimate interest is delivering this website securely and reliably. GitHub, Inc. participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer to the USA is covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). How long GitHub keeps this data is set out in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Visitor statistics: GoatCounter

To see which pages are visited, this site uses GoatCounter, operated by Martin Tournoij (Ireland). Data is stored on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany.

For each page view, GoatCounter receives the page address, the referring page, your browser's user agent, your screen width, your language and your IP address. From these it only keeps aggregate counts: page, referrer, browser, operating system, screen width, language and approximate location. GoatCounter does not store your IP address or the full user agent and sets no cookies. To avoid counting a repeat visit twice, it keeps a random ID derived from the site, your IP address and your user agent in memory for at most eight hours.

Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR: my legitimate interest is knowing how this site is used, using a privacy-friendly tool. You can object at any time (Art. 21 GDPR): open arveur.com/#toggle-goatcounter to stop being counted in your browser (this stores a single opt-out flag in your browser's local storage), or block the script with a content blocker. Details: GoatCounter privacy policy.

Data may be transferred to Apple Inc. in the USA on the basis of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR).

Legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your message concerns a contract or steps before one, and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR otherwise: my legitimate interest is answering your message. I delete correspondence once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods (for example under German tax law) require me to keep it.
